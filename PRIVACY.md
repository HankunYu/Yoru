# YORU Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

YORU is a video player. It does not collect, store on any server, or share any information about you.

## What YORU does not do

- No accounts and no sign-in.
- No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
- No third-party SDKs that gather data.
- Nothing is sent to the developer, at any time.

## What stays on your device

Everything YORU remembers is kept on your device, inside the app's own storage:

- Your videos, and the playback position of each.
- Thumbnails the app makes for your videos.
- Your settings, and the configuration files, scripts and shaders you add.
- The addresses and user names of the servers you add. Their passwords are kept in the iOS Keychain.
- A diagnostic log, written only if you turn diagnostics on in Settings. It stays in the app's folder and is never sent anywhere.

Deleting the app removes all of it.

## Network use

YORU connects only where you tell it to:

- The SMB and WebDAV servers you add, to list and play (or download) your files.
- Links you open in the app.

Those connections go from your device straight to that server. YORU asks for access to your local network for this purpose only.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

## Contact

Questions can be raised at <https://github.com/HankunYu/Yoru/issues>.

# YORU 隐私政策

最后更新:2026 年 11 月 13 日

YORU 是一个视频播放器。它不收集、不在任何服务器上保存、也不分享任何关于你的信息。

## YORU 不做的事

- 没有账号,不需要登录。
- 没有统计分析、追踪或广告。
- 没有收集数据的第三方 SDK。
- 任何时候都不会向开发者发送任何内容。

## 留在你设备上的内容

YORU 记住的一切都保存在你的设备上、app 自己的存储空间里:

- 你的视频,以及每个视频的播放进度。
- app 为视频生成的缩略图。
- 你的设置,以及你添加的配置文件、脚本和着色器。
- 你添加的服务器的地址和用户名。服务器密码保存在 iOS 钥匙串中。
- 诊断日志,只有在设置里打开诊断时才会写入。它留在 app 的文件夹里,不会发送到任何地方。

删除 app 会一并删除以上全部内容。

## 网络使用

YORU 只连接你让它连接的地方:

- 你添加的 SMB 和 WebDAV 服务器,用于列出、播放或下载你的文件。
- 你在 app 里打开的链接。

这些连接由你的设备直接发往对应的服务器。YORU 请求本地网络权限仅用于此目的。

## 变更

本政策如有变更,新版本会发布在同一地址并更新日期。

## 联系

有疑问可以在 <https://github.com/HankunYu/Yoru/issues> 提出。
